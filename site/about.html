About TestersHub

Real testers for Android apps going through Google Play's closed testing.

TestersHub helps Android app developers get their apps through Google Play's closed testing requirement and into production. We're based in Mumbai, India, and run by Gaurav Prasad, a QA automation engineer.

Since November 2023, new personal Google Play developer accounts must run a closed test with at least 12 testers for 14 days in a row before they can publish. Finding a dozen people who will genuinely use an app every day for two weeks is hard. That's what we do.

How we work

  • Real people with their own Google accounts and their own Android phones. Never emulators, bots or several accounts for one person.
  • Daily tasks, so your app is genuinely used for the full 14 days
  • Same-day replacement if a tester drops out
  • A feedback report with real bugs and suggestions, and draft answers for Google's production access questions

What we don't do

We don't sell fake installs, and we never promise that Google will approve an app. Google makes that decision. We promise genuine testing that gives your application the best chance.
