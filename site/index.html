TH-042 High severity

Promo code applied twice when "Pay now" is double-tapped

Platform
Android 14, Redmi Note 12, app v2.3.1
Area
Checkout, payments API

Steps to reproduce

  1. Add any item over ₹999 to the cart.
  2. Apply promo code SAVE20.
  3. Double-tap "Pay now" quickly on a slow 3G connection.

Expected

One discount, one order.

Actual

40% discount applied; two POST /orders requests sent.

Suggested fix: disable the button after the first tap and make the order endpoint idempotent.

Evidence: screen recording, network log

From requirement to release, in six steps

You approve what we test and what it costs. We tell you when it's safe to ship.

  1. Share

    Send whatever you have: a Jira ticket, a Figma design, a screen recording or a plain description. No formal spec needed.

  2. Plan

    We write the test cases and pick the depth: smoke, feature, regression or full regression. You approve the list and the price.

  3. Test

    We run every test case on real browsers and devices. Bugs go into your tracker as we find them, with steps and evidence.

  4. Report

    You get a release readiness report: what passed, what failed, what's still open, and our go or no-go recommendation.

  5. Retest

    Your team fixes the bugs, we retest every fix free, and we update the report until you're ready to ship.

  6. Automate optional

    Turn your approved test cases into Playwright, Selenium or Appium scripts that run on every commit in your CI/CD pipeline.

Common questions

What's included in the free trial?

We agree on one important flow in your product, such as sign-up or checkout, write and run the test cases for it, and send you a full report of what we find, with steps to reproduce. It's free, with no obligation, and available once per company.

Do you do manual or automated testing?

Both. Manual and exploratory testing finds the issues scripts miss; automation keeps your regression checks fast. Most projects use a mix.

Our app was built with AI coding tools. Can you test it?

Yes, and it's worth doing. AI-generated code often works on the happy path but fails on edge cases, permissions and security. Our pre-launch QA package is designed for exactly this.

Do you test on real phones?

Yes. We test on real Android and iOS devices and use a device cloud when you need wider model and OS coverage.

Will you work with our tools and pipeline?

Yes. We log bugs in your tracker and run automated suites in GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Jenkins, Azure DevOps or whatever you already use.

Can you sign an NDA?

Yes. We're happy to sign your NDA before we see your product or test environment.

What do you need from us to get started?

A link to your app or a test build, test accounts, and a description of what to test. That can be a Jira ticket, a Figma design, a screen recording or a few sentences. No formal specification needed.

How do we know when we're ready to release?

Every test cycle ends with a release readiness report: results for every test case, open bugs by severity, anything we couldn't test, and a clear go or no-go recommendation with the reasons.

How can I pay?

Online by UPI, debit or credit card, net banking or wallet through Razorpay, or by PayPal for international clients. Each invoice includes a secure payment link. See our payment page for terms.
