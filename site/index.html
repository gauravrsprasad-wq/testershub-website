Why closed tests fail, and how we prevent it

Most rejections come from a handful of avoidable mistakes. Our process is built around each one.

Testers drop below 12

If even one tester leaves before day 14, the clock restarts.

What we do: We start with 15 testers and replace anyone who goes quiet the same day.

Testers never properly opted in

People install from the store link instead of the opt-in link, so Google doesn't count them.

What we do: We check the opt-in list with you on day 1, tester by tester.

Installed, but never really used

Google looks for genuine use, not just installs.

What we do: Testers get short daily tasks, so your app is used across the whole 14 days.

Vague answers on the production access form

"Testers liked the app" doesn't show a real testing process.

What we do: You get a feedback report and draft answers with devices, issues found and what changed.

Questions developers ask

Does the 12-tester rule apply to my account?

It applies to personal Google Play developer accounts created after 13 November 2023. Organization accounts and older personal accounts are exempt. Use the checker above, or look at the Production access card on your Play Console dashboard.

Should I choose 12 or 15 testers?

Google needs at least 12 testers opted in for 14 days in a row. With 12, one dropout can restart that count; we replace testers the same day and keep going at no extra cost, but it can add days. With 15, three spare testers keep you above 12 even if someone drops out, so we recommend 15 if your launch date matters.

What happens if a tester drops out?

We check tester activity every day and bring in a replacement the same day. On the 15-tester plan your count stays above 12. On the 12-tester plan, if Google's count restarts because one of our testers left, we keep testing until you have 14 full days at no extra cost.

Can you guarantee that Google approves my app?

No one honestly can: Google makes that decision and also reviews your app itself. What we promise is genuine testing. If Google asks for more testing because tester engagement was too low, we run another 14 days free.

Are your testers real people on real phones?

Yes. Every tester is a real person with their own Google account and their own Android phone. We never use emulators, bots or several accounts for one person.

Is using a testing service allowed?

Google asks for real testers who genuinely use your app, and suggests recruiting through your networks and communities. We provide exactly that: real people who test your app properly for the full 14 days.

Do you need my source code?

No. We only need your closed testing opt-in link, a test login if your app has one, and a short note on what to focus on.

How do I pay?

Online by UPI, card, net banking or PayPal once we confirm your start date. See payment options and our refund policy.
