How payment works

  1. We agree the scope and send you a fixed quote by email.
  2. Once you accept, we email an invoice with its own secure payment link and an invoice number.
  3. Pay using that link, or with the buttons above, entering your invoice number.
  4. You receive a payment receipt by email, and work starts on the agreed date.
