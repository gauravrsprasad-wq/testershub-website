Which plan should I choose?

Google needs at least 12 testers opted in for 14 days in a row. With exactly 12, one dropout can restart that count. We replace testers the same day, and if the count restarts because one of our testers left, we keep testing until you have 14 full days at no extra cost. With 15 testers, the 3 spares keep you above 12 throughout, so we recommend 15 if your launch date matters.
