Cancellation and refund policy

Clear rules for cancellations and refunds, so there are no surprises.

Last updated: 26 September 2026

We want you to be confident booking with us. This policy explains what happens if you need to cancel or aren't happy with our work.

Free trial

The free trial costs nothing, so there's nothing to refund. It is available once per company.

Test packs ($10 for 20 test cases)

  • Cancelled before testing starts: full refund.
  • Cancelled during testing: any packs we haven't started are refunded.
  • After delivery: packs are not refunded, but if a result doesn't match the test case we agreed, tell us within 7 days and we'll rerun it free.

Custom projects (automation setup and other quoted work)

  • Cancelled before work starts: full refund of any advance paid.
  • Cancelled after work starts: you pay for the work completed up to the cancellation date, which we'll share with you in writing. Any advance above that amount is refunded.
  • After the final report or handover: no refund, but if you believe something we delivered doesn't match the agreed scope, tell us within 7 days and we'll fix it at no extra cost.

Dedicated QA (monthly)

  • Cancel any time with 15 days' written notice by email.
  • The current billing month is not refunded, but we won't bill for the following month after your notice.

If we cancel

If we can't start or complete agreed work for reasons on our side, we refund any amount paid for work not delivered in full.

How refunds are paid

  • Refunds are made to the original payment method.
  • We process approved refunds within 5 to 7 working days. Your bank or card provider may take a few extra days to show it.
  • Payment provider charges on international payments may not be recoverable.

How to request a cancellation or refund

Email gauravprasad@testershub.in with your invoice number and the reason. We'll confirm receipt within 2 working days.
