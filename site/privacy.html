Privacy policy

What personal data we collect, why, and your rights.

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This policy explains how TestersHub, operated by Gaurav Prasad from Mumbai, India, collects and uses personal data, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Enquiry form: your name, work email, company, the service you need and your message.
  • Email: anything you send us by email.
  • Payments: your name, email, amount and payment status. Card, UPI and bank details are handled by our payment providers (Razorpay and PayPal); we never see or store them.
  • Client projects: test accounts and data you give us to test your product.

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Why we use it

  • To reply to enquiries and prepare quotes
  • To deliver the services you've asked for
  • To send invoices, take payments and keep accounting records required by law

We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

  • Web3Forms: delivers enquiry form messages to our inbox
  • Zoho Mail: our business email
  • Razorpay and PayPal: process payments
  • Netlify: hosts this website

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't lead to a project: up to 12 months
  • Client test data and access: deleted or returned within 30 days of the project ending, or sooner on request
  • Invoices and payment records: as long as Indian tax law requires

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw your consent, by emailing gauravprasad@testershub.in. We will respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, see the grievance process on our contact page.

Security

We protect data with secure (HTTPS) connections, access controls and two-factor authentication on our accounts, and we only give access to people who need it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
